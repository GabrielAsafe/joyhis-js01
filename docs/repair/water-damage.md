# Water-Damage Repair

Water can create immediate shorts or delayed ionic corrosion. High-impedance SW, reset, feedback and UART nodes can fail even when power traces look intact.

## Procedure

1. Disconnect all power.
2. Photograph before cleaning.
3. Remove loose contamination.
4. Clean with high-purity isopropyl alcohol.
5. Brush connector pins and fine-pitch IC legs gently.
6. Inspect vias and traces under magnification.
7. Replace terminals with damaged plating.
8. Dry completely.
9. Repeat resistance checks before power.

Focus on the five-wire dashboard connector, U1 feedback network, MCU reset/clock region, GN16401 pins and any white/green residue.
